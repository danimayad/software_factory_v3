# Product brief: guide

This folder says what the product is. Every session and helper reads it, so it is
short, or it opens with a one-paragraph summary. It is written with Daniel.

It can be one document or several. What usually belongs here (a reminder, not a form;
add, drop or merge as the project needs):

- **Vision or product overview:** the general description.
- **Context, goals and users:** the nature of the project, why it exists, who it is for.
- **What matters most:** the priorities, and which one wins when two conflict.
- **Non-goals:** what the product deliberately does not do, and what is left open.

State what was decided as decided, and an idea or preference as such.
